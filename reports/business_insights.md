# Business Insights Report - Customer Churn Analysis

This report documents **22 detailed business insights** extracted from our end-to-end customer churn analysis, highlighting key risk groups and suggesting strategic marketing corrections.

---

## Executive Summary Baselines
- **Customer Base**: 7,043 total customers analyzed.
- **Retained Customer Count**: 5,174 active customers.
- **Churned Customer Count**: 1,869 customers lost.
- **Overall Churn Rate**: **26.54%** (Retention rate: **73.46%**).
- **Average Monthly Bill**: **$64.76**.
- **Monthly Revenue Lost**: **$139,130.85** due to churn.

---

## Detailed Business Insights

### 1. Contract Terms & Retention (High Priority)
1. **Month-to-Month Contract Vulnerability**: Customers on month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate by far, standing at **42.71%** (1,655 out of 3,875 customers).
2. **One-Year Contract Stability**: Upgrading a customer to a 1-Year contract slashes churn rate to **11.27%** (166 out of 1,473 customers).
3. **Two-Year Contract Loyalty**: Customers on 2-Year contracts represent the most loyal segment, with a negligible churn rate of only **2.83%** (48 out of 1,695 customers).
4. **Contract Transition Leverage**: Month-to-month contracts account for **88.5%** of all churned customers, indicating that a migration push from month-to-month to annual commitments is the single most effective churn-reduction strategy.

### 2. Service Quality & ISP Type
5. **Fiber Optic Churn Trap**: Fiber optic internet subscribers exhibit a highly concerning churn rate of **41.89%** (1,297 out of 3,096 customers), despite paying premium monthly fees.
6. **DSL Internet Stability**: In contrast, DSL internet subscribers are much more stable, exhibiting a churn rate of only **18.96%** (459 out of 2,421 customers).
7. **Value of Bundling (Optional Services)**: Customers who subscribe to **0** optional internet services (OnlineSecurity, TechSupport, etc.) exhibit a churn rate of **49.8%**. This drop rate plunges to **11.2%** for customers subscribing to **3** services, and to **3.5%** for customers subscribing to **5+** services.
8. **Tech Support Cushion**: Internet customers **without** Tech Support churn at **41.6%**, compared to just **15.2%** for those **with** Tech Support, showing the high retention value of customer assistance.
9. **Online Security Protection**: Internet customers **without** Online Security churn at **41.8%**, compared to just **14.6%** for those **with** Online Security.

### 3. Billing & Payment Methods
10. **Electronic Check Disproportion**: Customers using **Electronic Check** as their payment method exhibit a massive churn rate of **45.29%** (1,071 out of 2,365 customers).
11. **Paperless Billing Risk**: Customers with Paperless Billing enabled show a much higher churn rate (**33.56%**) compared to those receiving traditional paper statements (**16.33%**).
12. **Automatic Payment Loyalty**: Customers utilizing automatic payment options (Credit Card or Bank Transfer) have a combined churn rate of only **15.8%**, compared to a massive **38.4%** churn rate for customers using manual payments (Electronic/Mailed Check).
13. **Electronic Check Revenue Impact**: Out of the $139,130.85 monthly revenue lost to churn, **$79,250** (57%) is associated with customers paying via Electronic Check.

### 4. Demographic Cohorts
14. **Senior Citizen Risk**: Senior citizens show a significantly higher churn rate of **41.68%** (476 out of 1,142 customers) compared to non-seniors at **23.61%** (1,393 out of 5,901 customers).
15. **Single Customer Risk**: Customers without a partner churn at **32.96%**, compared to just **19.66%** for customers with partners.
16. **Independence/Dependents Impact**: Customers without dependents are twice as likely to churn (**31.28%**) compared to those with dependents (**15.45%**).
17. **Gender Neutrality**: Churn rates are nearly identical between genders (Females churn at **26.9%**, Males churn at **26.2%**), proving that churn is driven by service and contract factors, not gender.

### 5. Tenure Length & Pricing
18. **New Customer Fragility (Early Churn)**: Customers in their first 6 months have an alarming churn rate of **52.3%**, showing a major onboarding/retention gap.
19. **Long-Term Customer Retention**: Customers who reach a tenure of **60+ months** exhibit a minuscule churn rate of **6.6%**, displaying high customer lifetime stability.
20. **Monthly Charges Correlation**: Churned customers have a significantly higher average monthly bill of **$74.44** compared to active customers at **$61.27**.
21. **High Charges Density**: The highest density of churned customers is concentrated between **$75 and $95** per month, indicating that billing thresholds around $80 are sensitive pricing triggers.
22. **Estimated LTV Differential**: Two-year contract customers represent the highest Lifetime Value (**$3,710** average cumulative billing), compared to Month-to-month contracts at **$962** average cumulative billing.
